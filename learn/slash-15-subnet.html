/15 subnet (subnet mask 255.254.0.0)

A /15 is 131,072 IPv4 addresses, with subnet mask 255.254.0.0 and 131,070 usable hosts.

Subnet mask255.254.0.0
Wildcard mask0.1.255.255
Binary mask11111111.11111110.00000000.00000000
Total addresses131,072
Usable hosts131,070 (minus network and broadcast)
/24s in a /15512

What a /15 is used for

Two /16s. 198.18.0.0/15 is reserved for network benchmarking.

Where /15 subnets start

The block size is 2 in the second octet: a /15 always starts at 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, ….

Every /15 inside 10.0.0.0/8. The first 16 of 128 are shown.

Splitting and joining

A /15 splits into 2 /16s, 4 /17s, 8 /18s. Two /15s that sit side by side, starting on a multiple of 262,144 addresses, join into one /14.
