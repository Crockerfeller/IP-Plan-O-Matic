/12 subnet (subnet mask 255.240.0.0)

A /12 is 1,048,576 IPv4 addresses, with subnet mask 255.240.0.0 and 1,048,574 usable hosts.

Subnet mask255.240.0.0
Wildcard mask0.15.255.255
Binary mask11111111.11110000.00000000.00000000
Total addresses1,048,576
Usable hosts1,048,574 (minus network and broadcast)
/24s in a /124,096

What a /12 is used for

Sixteen /16s. 172.16.0.0/12 is one of the three private RFC 1918 blocks.

Where /12 subnets start

The block size is 16 in the second octet: a /12 always starts at 0, 16, 32, 48, 64, 80, ….

Splitting and joining

A /12 splits into 2 /13s, 4 /14s, 8 /15s. Two /12s that sit side by side, starting on a multiple of 2,097,152 addresses, join into one /11.
