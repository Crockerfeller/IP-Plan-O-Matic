IPv6 /56 prefix

A small site, branch office or home connection. Many ISPs give home customers a /56.

/64 networks inside256
Total addresses272 (4,722,366,482,869,645,213,696)
Example2001:db8:abcd:1200::/56

Splitting a /56

Split on nibble boundaries (multiples of 4 bits) so each level changes whole hex digits. The first /64s of 2001:db8:abcd:1200::/56:

/64
2001:db8:abcd:1200::/64
2001:db8:abcd:1201::/64
2001:db8:abcd:1202::/64
2001:db8:abcd:1203::/64
2001:db8:abcd:1204::/64
2001:db8:abcd:1205::/64
2001:db8:abcd:1206::/64
2001:db8:abcd:1207::/64
