/32 subnet (subnet mask 255.255.255.255)

A /32 is 1 IPv4 address, with subnet mask 255.255.255.255 and 1 usable host.

Subnet mask255.255.255.255
Wildcard mask0.0.0.0
Binary mask11111111.11111111.11111111.11111111
Total addresses1
Usable hosts1 (a single host)
/32s in a /24256

What a /32 is used for

A single address: loopbacks, host routes and firewall rules for one host.

Where /32 subnets start

Every address is its own /32.

Usable addresses in the cloud

Clouds reserve more addresses than the network and broadcast: 5 in AWS and Azure, 4 in Google Cloud, 3 in Oracle Cloud.
