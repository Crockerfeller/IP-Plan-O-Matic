/19 subnet (subnet mask 255.255.224.0)

A /19 is 8,192 IPv4 addresses, with subnet mask 255.255.224.0 and 8,190 usable hosts.

Subnet mask255.255.224.0
Wildcard mask0.0.31.255
Binary mask11111111.11111111.11100000.00000000
Total addresses8,192
Usable hosts8,190 (minus network and broadcast)
/24s in a /1932

What a /19 is used for

Thirty-two /24s: a medium VPC or a campus with many VLANs.

Where /19 subnets start

The block size is 32 in the third octet: a /19 always starts at 0, 32, 64, 96, 128, 160, ….

Usable addresses in the cloud

Clouds reserve more addresses than the network and broadcast: 5 in AWS and Azure, 4 in Google Cloud, 3 in Oracle Cloud.

Splitting and joining

A /19 splits into 2 /20s, 4 /21s, 8 /22s. Two /19s that sit side by side, starting on a multiple of 16,384 addresses, join into one /18.
