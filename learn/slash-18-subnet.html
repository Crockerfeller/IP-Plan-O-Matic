/18 subnet (subnet mask 255.255.192.0)

A /18 is 16,384 IPv4 addresses, with subnet mask 255.255.192.0 and 16,382 usable hosts.

Subnet mask255.255.192.0
Wildcard mask0.0.63.255
Binary mask11111111.11111111.11000000.00000000
Total addresses16,384
Usable hosts16,382 (minus network and broadcast)
/24s in a /1864

What a /18 is used for

A quarter of a /16, enough for a VPC with room for big subnets, or a building’s worth of VLANs.

Where /18 subnets start

The block size is 64 in the third octet: a /18 always starts at 0, 64, 128, 192.

Usable addresses in the cloud

Clouds reserve more addresses than the network and broadcast: 5 in AWS and Azure, 4 in Google Cloud, 3 in Oracle Cloud.

Splitting and joining

A /18 splits into 2 /19s, 4 /20s, 8 /21s. Two /18s that sit side by side, starting on a multiple of 32,768 addresses, join into one /17.
