Reading a prefix quickly

  • Each step down in prefix length doubles the size: a /23 is two /24s, a /22 is four.
  • The wildcard mask is the netmask inverted. ACLs on Cisco and some firewalls use it.
  • Within the last octet, block sizes are 128, 64, 32, 16, 8, 4, so a /26 always starts on .0, .64, .128 or .192.
