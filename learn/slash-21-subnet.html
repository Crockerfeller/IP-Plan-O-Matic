/21 subnet (subnet mask 255.255.248.0)

A /21 is 2,048 IPv4 addresses, with subnet mask 255.255.248.0 and 2,046 usable hosts.

Subnet mask255.255.248.0
Wildcard mask0.0.7.255
Binary mask11111111.11111111.11111000.00000000
Total addresses2,048
Usable hosts2,046 (minus network and broadcast)
/24s in a /218

What a /21 is used for

Eight /24s: a small VPC, or a large subnet for Kubernetes pods.

Where /21 subnets start

The block size is 8 in the third octet: a /21 always starts at 0, 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, ….

Every /21 inside 10.0.0.0/16. The first 16 of 32 are shown.

Splitting and joining

A /21 splits into 2 /22s, 4 /23s, 8 /24s. Two /21s that sit side by side, starting on a multiple of 4,096 addresses, join into one /20.
