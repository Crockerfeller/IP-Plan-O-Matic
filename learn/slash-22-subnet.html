/22 subnet (subnet mask 255.255.252.0)

A /22 is 1,024 IPv4 addresses, with subnet mask 255.255.252.0 and 1,022 usable hosts.

Subnet mask255.255.252.0
Wildcard mask0.0.3.255
Binary mask11111111.11111111.11111100.00000000
Total addresses1,024
Usable hosts1,022 (minus network and broadcast)
/24s in a /224

What a /22 is used for

Four /24s: 1,022 hosts. Used for big flat networks, Wi-Fi with many clients, and Kubernetes node subnets.

Where /22 subnets start

The block size is 4 in the third octet: a /22 always starts at 0, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, ….

Every /22 inside 10.0.0.0/16. The first 16 of 64 are shown.

Splitting and joining

A /22 splits into 2 /23s, 4 /24s, 8 /25s. Two /22s that sit side by side, starting on a multiple of 2,048 addresses, join into one /21.
