/11 subnet (subnet mask 255.224.0.0)

A /11 is 2,097,152 IPv4 addresses, with subnet mask 255.224.0.0 and 2,097,150 usable hosts.

Subnet mask255.224.0.0
Wildcard mask0.31.255.255
Binary mask11111111.11100000.00000000.00000000
Total addresses2,097,152
Usable hosts2,097,150 (minus network and broadcast)
/24s in a /118,192

What a /11 is used for

An eighth of a /8: a large regional or business-unit allocation.

Where /11 subnets start

The block size is 32 in the second octet: a /11 always starts at 0, 32, 64, 96, 128, 160, ….

Usable addresses in the cloud

Clouds reserve more addresses than the network and broadcast: 5 in AWS and Azure, 4 in Google Cloud, 3 in Oracle Cloud.

Splitting and joining

A /11 splits into 2 /12s, 4 /13s, 8 /14s. Two /11s that sit side by side, starting on a multiple of 4,194,304 addresses, join into one /10.
