/31 subnet (subnet mask 255.255.255.254)

A /31 is 2 IPv4 addresses, with subnet mask 255.255.255.254 and 2 usable hosts.

Subnet mask255.255.255.254
Wildcard mask0.0.0.1
Binary mask11111111.11111111.11111111.11111110
Total addresses2
Usable hosts2 (RFC 3021: both usable)
/31s in a /24128

What a /31 is used for

2 addresses, both usable: point-to-point links under RFC 3021, with no network or broadcast address.

Where /31 subnets start

The block size is 2 in the last octet: a /31 always starts at 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, ….

Every /31 inside 192.168.1.0/24. The first 16 of 128 are shown.

Usable addresses in the cloud

Clouds reserve more addresses than the network and broadcast: 5 in AWS and Azure, 4 in Google Cloud, 3 in Oracle Cloud.

CloudUsable addresses

Splitting and joining

A /31 splits into 2 /32s. Two /31s that sit side by side, starting on a multiple of 4 addresses, join into one /30.
