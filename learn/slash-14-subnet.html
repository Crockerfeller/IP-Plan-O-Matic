/14 subnet (subnet mask 255.252.0.0)

A /14 is 262,144 IPv4 addresses, with subnet mask 255.252.0.0 and 262,142 usable hosts.

Subnet mask255.252.0.0
Wildcard mask0.3.255.255
Binary mask11111111.11111100.00000000.00000000
Total addresses262,144
Usable hosts262,142 (minus network and broadcast)
/24s in a /141,024

What a /14 is used for

Four /16s: a mid-size region or a group of cloud networks.

Where /14 subnets start

The block size is 4 in the second octet: a /14 always starts at 0, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, ….

Every /14 inside 10.0.0.0/8. The first 16 of 64 are shown.

Splitting and joining

A /14 splits into 2 /15s, 4 /16s, 8 /17s. Two /14s that sit side by side, starting on a multiple of 524,288 addresses, join into one /13.
