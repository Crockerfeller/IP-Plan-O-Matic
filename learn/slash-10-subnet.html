/10 subnet (subnet mask 255.192.0.0)

A /10 is 4,194,304 IPv4 addresses, with subnet mask 255.192.0.0 and 4,194,302 usable hosts.

Subnet mask255.192.0.0
Wildcard mask0.63.255.255
Binary mask11111111.11000000.00000000.00000000
Total addresses4,194,304
Usable hosts4,194,302 (minus network and broadcast)
/24s in a /1016,384

What a /10 is used for

A quarter of a /8. 100.64.0.0/10 is the shared space ISPs use for carrier-grade NAT.

Where /10 subnets start

The block size is 64 in the second octet: a /10 always starts at 0, 64, 128, 192.

Usable addresses in the cloud

Clouds reserve more addresses than the network and broadcast: 5 in AWS and Azure, 4 in Google Cloud, 3 in Oracle Cloud.

Splitting and joining

A /10 splits into 2 /11s, 4 /12s, 8 /13s. Two /10s that sit side by side, starting on a multiple of 8,388,608 addresses, join into one /9.
