IPv6 /48 prefix

One site or campus. The usual allocation an organization gets for each location, and the size of a ULA prefix.

Splitting a /48

Split on nibble boundaries (multiples of 4 bits) so each level changes whole hex digits. The first /56s of fd12:3456:789a::/48:

/56
fd12:3456:789a::/56
fd12:3456:789a:100::/56
fd12:3456:789a:200::/56
fd12:3456:789a:300::/56
fd12:3456:789a:400::/56
fd12:3456:789a:500::/56
fd12:3456:789a:600::/56
fd12:3456:789a:700::/56
