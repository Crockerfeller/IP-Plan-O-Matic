/30 subnet (subnet mask 255.255.255.252)

A /30 is 4 IPv4 addresses, with subnet mask 255.255.255.252 and 2 usable hosts.

Subnet mask255.255.255.252
Wildcard mask0.0.0.3
Binary mask11111111.11111111.11111111.11111100
Total addresses4
Usable hosts2 (minus network and broadcast)
/30s in a /2464

What a /30 is used for

2 hosts: the classic router-to-router link. The smallest OCI subnet.

Where /30 subnets start

The block size is 4 in the last octet: a /30 always starts at 0, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, ….

Every /30 inside 192.168.1.0/24. The first 16 of 64 are shown.

Usable addresses in the cloud

Clouds reserve more addresses than the network and broadcast: 5 in AWS and Azure, 4 in Google Cloud, 3 in Oracle Cloud.

Splitting and joining

A /30 splits into 2 /31s, 4 /32s. Two /30s that sit side by side, starting on a multiple of 8 addresses, join into one /29.
