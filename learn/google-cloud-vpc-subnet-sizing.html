Google Cloud VPC subnet sizing

Google Cloud VPC networks are global and their subnets are regional. Each subnet loses 4 addresses to Google.

The 4 reserved addresses

In 10.2.0.0/24, Google keeps 10.2.0.0 (network), 10.2.0.1 (default gateway), 10.2.0.254 (second-to-last, reserved for future use) and 10.2.0.255 (broadcast).

Auto mode or custom mode

An auto mode network creates one subnet per region from 10.128.0.0/9. That’s convenient but hard to connect to other networks without overlaps. Custom mode lets you choose every range, and is what you want for anything beyond a test project.

Growing a subnet

You can expand a subnet’s primary range to a shorter prefix later without downtime, as long as the larger range doesn’t overlap anything. You can’t shrink it. So start small enough to leave neighbors room, and plan where each subnet would grow.

Secondary ranges for GKE

GKE VPC-native clusters take pod and service addresses from secondary ranges on the subnet. Pod ranges get big (by default each node reserves a /24 for pods), so reserve space for them in your plan.

The planner applies Google Cloud’s reserved addresses and /29 minimum, and exports Terraform for google_compute_subnetwork.
