/20 subnet (subnet mask 255.255.240.0)

A /20 is 4,096 IPv4 addresses, with subnet mask 255.255.240.0 and 4,094 usable hosts.

Subnet mask255.255.240.0
Wildcard mask0.0.15.255
Binary mask11111111.11111111.11110000.00000000
Total addresses4,096
Usable hosts4,094 (minus network and broadcast)
/24s in a /2016

What a /20 is used for

Sixteen /24s: a common size for a spoke VNet or VPC, or a mid-size branch.

Where /20 subnets start

The block size is 16 in the third octet: a /20 always starts at 0, 16, 32, 48, 64, 80, ….

Splitting and joining

A /20 splits into 2 /21s, 4 /22s, 8 /23s. Two /20s that sit side by side, starting on a multiple of 8,192 addresses, join into one /19.
