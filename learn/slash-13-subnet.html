/13 subnet (subnet mask 255.248.0.0)

A /13 is 524,288 IPv4 addresses, with subnet mask 255.248.0.0 and 524,286 usable hosts.

Subnet mask255.248.0.0
Wildcard mask0.7.255.255
Binary mask11111111.11111000.00000000.00000000
Total addresses524,288
Usable hosts524,286 (minus network and broadcast)
/24s in a /132,048

What a /13 is used for

Eight /16s, often a large region’s share of a 10.0.0.0/8 plan.

Where /13 subnets start

The block size is 8 in the second octet: a /13 always starts at 0, 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, ….

Every /13 inside 10.0.0.0/8. The first 16 of 32 are shown.

Splitting and joining

A /13 splits into 2 /14s, 4 /15s, 8 /16s. Two /13s that sit side by side, starting on a multiple of 1,048,576 addresses, join into one /12.
