/23 subnet (subnet mask 255.255.254.0)

A /23 is 512 IPv4 addresses, with subnet mask 255.255.254.0 and 510 usable hosts.

Subnet mask255.255.254.0
Wildcard mask0.0.1.255
Binary mask11111111.11111111.11111110.00000000
Total addresses512
Usable hosts510 (minus network and broadcast)
/24s in a /232

What a /23 is used for

Two /24s: 510 hosts. A large office LAN or a guest Wi-Fi network.

Where /23 subnets start

The block size is 2 in the third octet: a /23 always starts at 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, ….

Every /23 inside 10.0.0.0/16. The first 16 of 128 are shown.

Usable addresses in the cloud

Clouds reserve more addresses than the network and broadcast: 5 in AWS and Azure, 4 in Google Cloud, 3 in Oracle Cloud.

Splitting and joining

A /23 splits into 2 /24s, 4 /25s, 8 /26s. Two /23s that sit side by side, starting on a multiple of 1,024 addresses, join into one /22.
