/28 subnet (subnet mask 255.255.255.240)

A /28 is 16 IPv4 addresses, with subnet mask 255.255.255.240 and 14 usable hosts.

Subnet mask255.255.255.240
Wildcard mask0.0.0.15
Binary mask11111111.11111111.11111111.11110000
Total addresses16
Usable hosts14 (minus network and broadcast)
/28s in a /2416

What a /28 is used for

14 hosts: the smallest AWS subnet, and a common size for Transit Gateway attachments and small DMZs.

Where /28 subnets start

The block size is 16 in the last octet: a /28 always starts at 0, 16, 32, 48, 64, 80, ….

Usable addresses in the cloud

Clouds reserve more addresses than the network and broadcast: 5 in AWS and Azure, 4 in Google Cloud, 3 in Oracle Cloud.

Splitting and joining

A /28 splits into 2 /29s, 4 /30s, 8 /31s. Two /28s that sit side by side, starting on a multiple of 32 addresses, join into one /27.
