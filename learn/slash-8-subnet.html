/8 subnet (subnet mask 255.0.0.0)

A /8 is 16,777,216 IPv4 addresses, with subnet mask 255.0.0.0 and 16,777,214 usable hosts.

Subnet mask255.0.0.0
Wildcard mask0.255.255.255
Binary mask11111111.00000000.00000000.00000000
Total addresses16,777,216
Usable hosts16,777,214 (minus network and broadcast)
/24s in a /865,536

What a /8 is used for

A whole former class A network. 10.0.0.0/8 is the largest private block, usually carved into a /16 per region, site or cloud.

Where /8 subnets start

A /8 ends on an octet boundary, so its subnets differ in the first octet and the octets after it are 0.

Usable addresses in the cloud

Clouds reserve more addresses than the network and broadcast: 5 in AWS and Azure, 4 in Google Cloud, 3 in Oracle Cloud.

Splitting and joining

A /8 splits into 2 /9s, 4 /10s, 8 /11s. Two /8s that sit side by side, starting on a multiple of 33,554,432 addresses, join into one /7.
