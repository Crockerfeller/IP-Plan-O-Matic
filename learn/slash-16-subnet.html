/16 subnet (subnet mask 255.255.0.0)

A /16 is 65,536 IPv4 addresses, with subnet mask 255.255.0.0 and 65,534 usable hosts.

Subnet mask255.255.0.0
Wildcard mask0.0.255.255
Binary mask11111111.11111111.00000000.00000000
Total addresses65,536
Usable hosts65,534 (minus network and broadcast)
/24s in a /16256

What a /16 is used for

The classic site or cloud network size: 65,536 addresses. The largest AWS VPC CIDR block and the usual size for a whole VNet or VPC.

Where /16 subnets start

A /16 ends on an octet boundary, so its subnets differ in the second octet and the octets after it are 0.

10.0.0.0/16 itself.

Usable addresses in the cloud

Clouds reserve more addresses than the network and broadcast: 5 in AWS and Azure, 4 in Google Cloud, 3 in Oracle Cloud.

CloudUsable addresses
AWS VPC65,531
Azure VNet65,531
Google Cloud VPC65,532
Oracle Cloud VCN65,533

Splitting and joining

A /16 splits into 2 /17s, 4 /18s, 8 /19s. Two /16s that sit side by side, starting on a multiple of 131,072 addresses, join into one /15.
