/27 subnet (subnet mask 255.255.255.224)

A /27 is 32 IPv4 addresses, with subnet mask 255.255.255.224 and 30 usable hosts.

Subnet mask255.255.255.224
Wildcard mask0.0.0.31
Binary mask11111111.11111111.11111111.11100000
Total addresses32
Usable hosts30 (minus network and broadcast)
/27s in a /248

What a /27 is used for

30 hosts: small VLANs, management networks, and Azure GatewaySubnet (/27 recommended).

Where /27 subnets start

The block size is 32 in the last octet: a /27 always starts at 0, 32, 64, 96, 128, 160, ….

Usable addresses in the cloud

Clouds reserve more addresses than the network and broadcast: 5 in AWS and Azure, 4 in Google Cloud, 3 in Oracle Cloud.

Splitting and joining

A /27 splits into 2 /28s, 4 /29s, 8 /30s. Two /27s that sit side by side, starting on a multiple of 64 addresses, join into one /26.
