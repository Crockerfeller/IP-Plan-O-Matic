/29 subnet (subnet mask 255.255.255.248)

A /29 is 8 IPv4 addresses, with subnet mask 255.255.255.248 and 6 usable hosts.

Subnet mask255.255.255.248
Wildcard mask0.0.0.7
Binary mask11111111.11111111.11111111.11111000
Total addresses8
Usable hosts6 (minus network and broadcast)
/29s in a /2432

What a /29 is used for

6 hosts: the smallest Azure and Google Cloud subnet, a small public IP block from an ISP, or a tiny DMZ.

Where /29 subnets start

The block size is 8 in the last octet: a /29 always starts at 0, 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, ….

Every /29 inside 192.168.1.0/24. The first 16 of 32 are shown.

Usable addresses in the cloud

Clouds reserve more addresses than the network and broadcast: 5 in AWS and Azure, 4 in Google Cloud, 3 in Oracle Cloud.

Splitting and joining

A /29 splits into 2 /30s, 4 /31s, 8 /32s. Two /29s that sit side by side, starting on a multiple of 16 addresses, join into one /28.
